Chapter 1

Chemical Reactions & Equations

Combination Exothermic

Burning of Magnesium

2Mg(s) + O2(g) → 2MgO(s)

🔬 Observations

  • Bright dazzling white light emitted
  • White fumes of MgO formed
  • Intense heat released (exothermic)
  • White powdery residue left behind
Decomposition Thermal

Decomposition of Ferrous Sulphate

2FeSO4(s) →Δ Fe2O3(s) + SO2(g) + SO3(g)

🔬 Observations

  • Green crystals turn reddish-brown (Fe₂O₃)
  • Smell of burning sulphur (SO₂ gas)
  • Colourless gas SO₃ also released
Decomposition Thermal

Decomposition of Lead Nitrate

2Pb(NO3)2(s) →Δ 2PbO(s) + 4NO2(g) + O2(g)

🔬 Observations

  • Brown fumes of NO₂ gas evolve
  • White powder turns yellow (PbO)
  • Crackling sound heard
  • O₂ gas also released
Decomposition Electrolytic

Electrolysis of Water

2H2O(l) →electricity 2H2(g) + O2(g)

🔬 Observations

  • H₂ gas at cathode (–): double volume
  • O₂ gas at anode (+): single volume
  • Volume ratio H₂:O₂ = 2:1
  • Water level gradually decreases
Displacement

Iron Nail in Copper Sulphate

Fe(s) + CuSO4(aq) → FeSO4(aq) + Cu(s)

🔬 Observations

  • Blue CuSO₄ solution turns pale green (FeSO₄)
  • Iron nail gets reddish-brown copper coating
  • Iron is more reactive than copper
Double Displacement Precipitation

BaCl₂ + Na₂SO₄ → White Precipitate

BaCl2(aq) + Na2SO4(aq) → BaSO4(s)↓ + 2NaCl(aq)

🔬 Observations

  • White precipitate of BaSO₄ forms immediately
  • Insoluble in dilute acids
  • Ions exchange between salts
Chapter 2

Acids, Bases & Salts

Acid + Metal

Zinc + Dilute Sulphuric Acid

Zn(s) + H2SO4(aq) → ZnSO4(aq) + H2(g)↑

🔬 Observations

  • Hydrogen gas bubbles vigorously
  • Zinc granules gradually dissolve
  • Burns with a pop (H₂ is combustible)
Neutralization Exothermic

NaOH + HCl Neutralization

NaOH(aq) + HCl(aq) → NaCl(aq) + H2O(l)

🔬 Observations

  • Pink (phenolphthalein) turns colourless at neutral
  • pH changes from acidic → neutral (7)
  • Temperature of solution rises (exothermic)
Acid + Carbonate

Na₂CO₃ + HCl → CO₂

Na2CO3(s) + 2HCl(aq) → 2NaCl(aq) + H2O(l) + CO2(g)↑

🔬 Observations

  • Vigorous effervescence of CO₂ gas
  • CO₂ turns limewater milky
  • Salt and water also formed
Interactive

pH Scale — Universal Indicator

pH = −log[H+]    Acidic < 7 < Basic

🔬 pH Examples

  • HCl: pH ≈ 1 (strong acid)
  • Pure Water: pH = 7 (neutral)
  • NaOH: pH ≈ 14 (strong base)
Combination Exothermic

Quicklime + Water (Slaking)

CaO(s) + H2O(l) → Ca(OH)2(aq) + Heat

🔬 Observations

  • Hissing sound when water is added
  • Vigorous steam/vapour rises
  • Temperature rises sharply
  • Slaked lime Ca(OH)₂ is formed
Chapter 3

Metals & Non-Metals

Metal + Water Vigorous

Sodium + Water

2Na(s) + 2H2O(l) → 2NaOH(aq) + H2(g) + Heat

🔬 Observations

  • Sodium skates/slides on water surface
  • H₂ gas catches fire — lilac/yellow flame
  • Very vigorous, exothermic reaction
  • NaOH solution formed (turns phenolphthalein pink)
Metal + Steam

Iron + Steam

3Fe(s) + 4H2O(g) →Δ Fe3O4(s) + 4H2(g)

🔬 Observations

  • Iron wool/strip turns black (Fe₃O₄)
  • H₂ gas collected at outlet
  • Reaction needs high temperature (steam)
Displacement Highly Exothermic

Thermite Reaction

Fe2O3(s) + 2Al(s) →Δ Al2O3(s) + 2Fe(l) + Heat

🔬 Observations

  • Intense dazzling light and heat
  • Molten iron (Fe) flows out
  • Aluminium displaces iron from its oxide
  • Used in welding railway tracks
Oxidation Slow Reaction

Corrosion of Iron (Rusting)

4Fe(s) + 3O2(g) + xH2O → 2Fe2O3·xH2O(rust)

🔬 Observations

  • Iron surface gradually turns reddish-brown
  • Rust = hydrated iron oxide (Fe₂O₃·xH₂O)
  • Requires O₂ and moisture
  • Prevented by painting, galvanizing, alloying
Chapter 4

Carbon and its Compounds

Combustion Highly Exothermic

Combustion of Methane

CH4(g) + 2O2(g) → CO2(g) + 2H2O(g) + Heat + Light

🔬 Observations

  • Burns with a clean blue flame
  • Releases large amount of heat and light
  • Produces carbon dioxide and water vapour
Oxidation

Oxidation of Ethanol

CH3CH2OH →Alk. KMnO₄ + Δ CH3COOH

🔬 Observations

  • Purple colour of KMnO₄ disappears initially
  • Ethanol is oxidised to Ethanoic acid
  • Requires heat for the reaction to proceed
Addition Catalytic

Addition Reaction (Hydrogenation)

Vegetable Oil (Unsaturated) + H2 →Ni catalyst Animal Fat (Saturated)

🔬 Observations

  • Liquid vegetable oil solidifies into fat (ghee)
  • Hydrogen is added across the double bond
  • Nickel or Palladium acts as a catalyst
Substitution

Substitution in Sunlight

CH4 + Cl2 →Sunlight CH3Cl + HCl

🔬 Observations

  • Yellow-green chlorine gas reacts with methane
  • Colour of chlorine fades in sunlight
  • Hydrogen is substituted by chlorine atom
Displacement

Ethanol + Sodium

2Na + 2CH3CH2OH → 2CH3CH2ONa + H2↑

🔬 Observations

  • Sodium metal reacts steadily with ethanol
  • Bubbles of hydrogen gas are evolved
  • Sodium ethoxide solution is formed
Esterification

Reaction to form Ester

CH3COOH + C2H5OH →Acid CH3COOC2H5 + H2O

🔬 Observations

  • Ethanoic acid and ethanol react upon heating
  • A sweet-smelling substance (ester) is formed
  • Requires an acid catalyst (conc. H₂SO₄)
Saponification

Saponification (Soap formation)

CH3COOC2H5 + NaOH → CH3COONa + C2H5OH

🔬 Observations

  • Ester reacts with a base (NaOH)
  • Sodium salt of carboxylic acid (soap) is formed
  • Ethanol is generated as a byproduct
Acid + Carbonate

Ethanoic Acid + Sodium Carbonate

2CH3COOH + Na2CO3 → 2CH3COONa + H2O + CO2↑

🔬 Observations

  • Brisk effervescence occurs immediately
  • Carbon dioxide gas is rapidly released
  • Sodium ethanoate salt is formed
